## Questions for the consultation of municipalities

1. **Reference levels:** which of the methods is most efficient in daily practice?
2. **Methods per check:** are the described check procedures complete and correct?
3. **Legal references:** are the cited articles correct?
4. **Feasibility:** does delivery through this ILS fit into your current permitting process?
