This guide describes how municipalities can assess building permit applications using a combination of BIM models (IFC) and geo-information: **GeoBIM**. The toolkit was developed within VNG Policy Measure 13 and works out seventeen common checks, from use function and building height in the environment plan to fire compartmentation and daylight in the Buildings (Living Environment) Decree (Bbl).

For each check, the toolkit shows what the rule means, how the check is carried out today, which BIM and geodata are needed, and how the rule is made machine-readable step by step. The toolkit also describes the architecture in which this can work nationally: from laws and regulations and organisation to information, applications and infrastructure.

The toolkit is intended for permit officers, information managers and architects at municipalities, and for software vendors who want to include the checks in their products.
