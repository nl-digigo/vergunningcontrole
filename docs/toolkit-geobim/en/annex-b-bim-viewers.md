# List of BIM viewer software {#bim-viewers}

Would your municipality like to get started with reviewing a BIM model? A BIM viewer is a good place to begin.

Below is an overview of commonly used BIM viewers with which municipalities can open and explore an IFC file. Some of these viewers are free for a limited time or under certain conditions.

| # | Viewer | Licence | Notes |
|---|--------|----------|-------------|
| 1 | [BIM Vision](https://bimvision.eu/) | Free | Freeware IFC model viewer |
| 2 | [BIMcollab Zoom](https://www.bimcollab.com/en/downloads/) | Free / paid | Desktop viewer with free and paid versions |
| 3 | [Solibri Model Checker](https://www.solibri.com/) | Paid | Desktop BIM model checker |
| 4 | [BIM Base](https://bim.works/) | Paid | BIM.works |
| 5 | [ACCA software](https://www.accasoftware.com/en/) | Paid | Architecture, engineering and construction |
| 6 | [Sortdesk](https://viewer.sortdesk.com/) | Free | Free online IFC viewer — works very well |
| 7 | [Blender with the Bonsai add-on](https://bonsaibim.org/) | Free | Open source, data-rich OpenBIM |
| 8 | [Qonic](https://www.qonic.com/) | Paid | Cloud-based BIM modelling platform |
| 9 | [BIM.permit](https://vsk-software.com/en/bim-permit/) | Paid | VSK.software |
| 10 | [BEXEL Manager](https://bexelmanager.com/) | Paid | BIM management and model analysis |
| 11 | [BIMsync / Catenda Hub](https://hub.catenda.com/signin) | Free / paid | Cloud environment for sharing and viewing |
